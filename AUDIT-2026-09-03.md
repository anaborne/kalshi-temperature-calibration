on 2026-09-03 against commit `4ee3b84`, and the two rounds of fixes that

defects were raised across the two passes; twenty-three are closed in the
current tree, and the last of them, along with one further defect found while
confirming the fixes, is closed by a third round of fixes on 2026-09-04.

## What was wrong and what changed

### 1. Settle accuracy was the bucket's Yes rate

`test_b.py` scored `in_bucket(settled, bounds)` for every shape. That flag is the
bucket settling Yes, which is the trade winning on a buy and the trade losing on
a sell. The dominated-bucket sell row published 94.9% as the rate the trade had
to clear, against the 98.1% breakeven §6 derives for a 2¢ bucket.

The statistic is now side-aware and the breakeven is computed from the prices the
book filled at. `README.md` labels the column as the bucket's Yes rate and reads
the sell row as a 5.1% settle-No rate against about 29% needed at the ~73¢ mean
fill. `RESULTS.md` §7 carries a dated correction. The committed logs predate the
code fix and say so in `scratch/README.md`.

### 2. The pre-period did not precede the backtest

`PREPERIOD_END` was 2025-01-01, chosen while the market archive was believed to
reach back 19 months. The pull reached 2021-08-06, so at least 861 of the
holdout's 1,463 trade dates fell inside the fit window and were priced in part by
their own observations. `verify_preperiod()` asserted only that no observation on
or after the cutoff entered the table.

`PREPERIOD_END` is 2021-08-01, and `verify_preperiod()` now exits if the earliest
market date falls inside the fit window. `PREREGISTRATION.md` carries Correction
4. `check_depth_bias.py` stopped reading the constant and names its own era end,
so the §3 figures did not move underneath the change.

### 3. The per-bin claim was never computed

`check_calibration.py` printed "the quote is a better forecast than the model at
every probability bin" whenever the aggregate Brier favoured the market. No
per-bin comparison existed in the pipeline.

`per_bin_calibration()` now computes the absolute calibration error inside each
forecaster's own bins and prints the count it finds. On the committed
`figures/calibration.json` the quote is closer in eleven of twelve bins and the
model is closer in the 0.30-0.40 bin, where the model is off by 0.026 and the
quote by 0.048. `README.md`, the figure title, `RESULTS.md` §6 and
`PREREGISTRATION.md` all state eleven of twelve.

### 4. README described the table as fit on training cities only

Correction 2 voids that clause and `cmd_fit` fits every city from its own
observations. The README restated the voided clause as the method.

README:45 now describes the table as fit on each city's own observations from a
frozen pre-period, names Correction 2, and keeps the training-city freeze on the
trading rule where it belongs.

### 5. The noise floor was measured on the wrong sample shape

The null split the modern era into two disjoint halves of about 2,739 days each.
The comparison it judged is a table on about 10,958 days against a table on about
5,479 days drawn from inside it. The sampling spread of a per-cell difference is
about 2.8 times wider under the disjoint split for the 1995 column and about 4.0
times wider for the 2005 column.

`check_depth_bias.py` now builds the null at the sizes and the nesting of the
comparison it benchmarks. `RESULTS.md` §3.1 carries a dated correction giving
rescaled floors of 3.90¢, 4.23¢, 3.76¢, 4.08¢ and 3.93¢ against the 1995 column,
with all five observed differences above them, and records that the retraction of
the 7.47¢ NYC citation does not stand.

### 6. The candlestick count rested on a missing commit

RESULTS §1 rested the 60,906 / 60,906 count on `fetch_candles.py --status` output
and on the commit message of the commit that completed the cache. Neither is in
this repository.

A dated correction states that the final count has no committed source and points
to `scratch/fetch_candles.log`, which ends at 60,753 cached with 153 markets
errored on HTTP 429.

### 7. METHOD.md miscounts the corrections in RESULTS.md

METHOD.md said `RESULTS.md` carried two dated corrections when it carried three.
It then said nine. `RESULTS.md` carried nine after the first round of fixes and
eleven after the second, and METHOD.md was not revisited. `grep -c "Corrected
20" RESULTS.md` returns 11, and METHOD.md now says eleven. The count of four
numbered corrections and three dated notes in `PREREGISTRATION.md` is right.

### 8. A retracted pairing was live in two source comments

`test_b.py` and `check_calibration.py` carried "+0.8 °F with sd 0.74", a pairing
that `RESULTS.md` §1 and `PREREGISTRATION.md` Correction 3 both retract.

`test_b.py:99` gives +0.72 °F with sd 0.74 on the NWS stratum and +0.86 / 0.67 on
TWC, matching `scratch/test_a.log:24,26`. `check_calibration.py:21` gives roughly
+0.7 °F.

### 9. The clustered interval for the two shapes was never printed

`RESULTS.md` §8 and `README.md` said every interval clears zero on both
bootstraps. `by_shape()` computed the clustered interval for each shape and
printed only the i.i.d. one, so no committed log carries it for the two shapes.

`by_shape()` prints the clustered interval on its own line. README scopes the
two-bootstrap claim to the four seasons and cites `test_b_holdout.log:23-30`, and
`RESULTS.md` §8 carries a dated correction.

### 10. Position size uses the completed hour's volume

Size is 5% of hour H's total volume, which is known once hour H has closed, and
the lag-1 sensitivity re-lags `M_H` alone. The limitations block did not name it.

It is the third entry in that block, which is now headed correctly.

### 11. stations.py matched one settlement wording of three

The sample carries three wordings of the same NWS product and `test_a.py` pools
all three. `stations.py` matched only "Climatological Report", which would leave
3,730 NWS city-days with no settlement authority in the mapping table and in the
`authority_spans` it writes.

`stations.py` uses the pattern `test_a.py` uses. Both match all three wordings.

### 12. The NYC density pair had no committed source

`check_depth_bias.py` fixes its eras at 1995-2009 and 2010-2024, so nothing in
this repository measures a density break at 2005.

A dated correction in `RESULTS.md` §3.3 and the comment in `fetch_obs.py` both
give 26.1 → 30.7 obs/day across the era boundary, cited to
`scratch/check_depth_bias.log:41`. The +0.122 °F, the t of +4.5 and the −0.013¢
of signed fair value were checked and stand.

### 13. "57/43" is the city ratio

A dated correction inside Correction 1 gives 816 / 551 city-days on the live tier
and 64.5/35.5 over the full 10,677 settled city-days.

### R2-3. Two weightings were compared against each other

`settle_accuracy_pct` was weighted by trades and `breakeven_accuracy_pct` and

and the difference between the two now equals the cents-per-contract figure. A
second dated correction in `RESULTS.md` §7 records that the published 5.1% is
trade-weighted, that this shape holds 1,369 contracts over 78 trades, and that
the per-trade sizes live in `data/test_b_results.json`, which is not committed.

### R2-9. The 2005 column sat inside its own error bars

The correction now says the rescaled floors run 2.7¢ to 3.0¢, close enough to the
observed differences that the column is not decided either way until the table is
re-run. The 1995 column keeps its conclusion, which has headroom.

## What was checked and found correct

Against a fresh clone of `1563b39`, on Python 3.11.15:

- `python make_figures.py` regenerates `figures/calibration.svg` byte for byte.
  The SHA-256 before and after the run is
  `b81a13a0afe829243fe9fe76301ec5f2aee4b05b18b06bae259cb4c024a8da65`, and
  `git status` is clean afterwards. The figure carries the corrected title.
- `python -m compileall .` reports no error on any module.
- Every analysis script dies on the missing `data/` cache and none dies at
  import. `buckets.py`, `test_a.py` and `check_calibration.py` raise
  `FileNotFoundError` on a named path under `data/`; `fetch_candles.py` and
  `stations.py` print the missing-file message from `common`; `test_b.py --fit`
  and `--verify` stop at `data/obs/MDW.csv`, and `--holdout` refuses to run
  without a frozen rule. `fetch_markets.py` and `fetch_obs.py` are the two
  network pulls and both answer `--help` without touching the cache.
- The headline Brier pair reproduces from `figures/calibration.json`: model
  0.17389841888, market 0.07719095069, both over 235,145 bucket-hours, with an
  identical base rate of 0.25255906 on the two reliability series.
- The per-bin count reproduces. The market's absolute calibration error is
  smaller in eleven of twelve bins and larger in 0.30-0.40, at 0.0479 against the
  model's 0.0264.
- The train and holdout assignment reproduces from `md5(series_ticker)` low bit
  for all 21 series, and the twelve train series sum to 4,896 settled markets and
  the nine holdout series to 3,306, which are 816 and 551 city-days at six per
  ladder and split 59.7/40.3.
- The 10,677 settled city-days split 6,889 / 3,788 by the same assignment over
  `scratch/fetch_hist2.log:75-95`, which is 64.5/35.5.
- The dominated-bucket sell arithmetic holds. 78 trades at −$4.1176 is −$321.17,
  which at −23.46¢/contract is 1,369 contracts and a mean size of 17.6. A 94.9%
  Yes rate against −23.46¢/contract puts the mean fill near 73¢, where the fee
  model gives a breakeven near 28.5% settle-No.
- The worked example in the second §7 correction is exact under the repository's
  own fee model. Forty sells at 73¢, twenty of size 1 settling No and twenty of
  size 100 settling Yes, return −27.39¢/contract against a 28.4% breakeven and a
  trade-weighted 50.0%.
- The contract-weighted statistic has the property the fix claims: for either
  side, contract-weighted settle rate minus contract-weighted breakeven equals
  total PnL divided by contracts.
- The rescaling ratios check out. Two disjoint halves of 2,739 give 0.0270 sigma;
  the nested pair at 10,958 and 5,479 gives 0.00955, a ratio of 2.83; the nested
  pair at 7,305 and 5,479 gives 0.00675, a ratio of 4.00.
- Every log citation added or moved by the two fix rounds resolves to the stated
  line and the stated number, including `check_calibration.log:5,44,45,60-63`,
  `test_b_holdout.log:13-15,17,23-30,32,46-47,50-53`, `test_b_fit.log:44,46,48`,
  `fetch_hist2.log:67,75-95,99`, `fetch_obs_totals.log:2-22,24`,
  `check_depth_bias.log:41,96` and `test_a.log:4,24,26`.
- `scratch/README.md` names the four logs whose output the code no longer
  produces, and each of those four is covered by a dated correction.
- The corrections in the first round were appended and nothing above them was
  touched: `RESULTS.md` gained 80 lines and removed none, `PREREGISTRATION.md`
  gained 26 and removed none.

One departure from the repository's own convention is on the record here. The
second round edited five previously published passages in place inside
`PREREGISTRATION.md` and `RESULTS.md`, all of them inside corrections dated
2026-09-03 and published earlier the same day: the candlestick correction, the

city-day figures inside Correction 1's dated note, and the calendar-day and

is never edited in place. The two additions the second round made to the same

## What remains unverifiable

The `data/` cache is not committed and the pull is on the order of 10⁴ requests
plus 6.8 million ASOS rows, so the published run cannot be reproduced from this
repository. Every number in `RESULTS.md` was checked against the committed logs
and `figures/calibration.json` and against itself, and none of it was recomputed
from source data. Several of the corrections dated 2026-09-03 describe a defect
that a future run will fix and that this run's numbers still carry: the
pre-period moves to 2021-08-01, and the published sample was produced under
2025-01-01; the settle rate becomes side-aware and contract-weighted, and the
published 5.1% is a trade-weighted rate whose contract weighting cannot be
recovered because the per-trade sizes are in an uncommitted file; the noise floor
is rebuilt at the comparison's own shape, and the §3.1 table is the old floor.
The trade tape behind −8.75¢/contract, −$27,039.23 and 19,348 trades is not
committed either, so those figures are internally consistent with the logs and
not independently derivable. The pre-registration timestamps are stated and not
checkable, because the repository was republished as a single commit on
2026-08-29.
